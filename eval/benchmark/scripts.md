# Benchmark scripts

Read each script as an MC would at a launching: clearly, with energy, at your
normal pace. **Leave a pause of about five seconds between numbered lines.**
The words in brackets are stage directions, not to be read. Small slips are
fine and realistic; do not re-record for them, and note any slip in the
recording log so the answer key can be checked honestly.

Each file must be under **two minutes**. Record mono or stereo WAV (16-bit
PCM, any sample rate). Name files `<speaker>-<script>-<condition>.wav`, for
example `ada-A-quiet.wav`, `ada-A-music.wav`, `tobi-C-quiet.wav`.

For the `music` condition, play praise or highlife music from a phone or
speaker about two metres from the microphone, at a level where you still
have to raise your voice a little.

## Script A — opening pledges

1. Let us begin the giving. Chief Emeka Okonkwo, two hundred and fifty thousand naira! Clap for him!
2. Deaconess Ngozi Eze, one hundred thousand naira.
3. Mr. Chinedu Okafor, fifty thousand naira.
4. Mrs. Funmilayo Adeyemi, seventy-five thousand naira.
5. Alhaji Ibrahim Musa, fifty thousand — sorry, seventy thousand naira.
6. Dr. Tola Adeyemi, one million naira! God bless you, sir.
7. Pastor Segun Ogunleye, twenty thousand naira.
8. Engineer Tunde Bakare, one hundred and fifty thousand naira.

## Script B — crowd moments

1. Barrister Kemi Oladipo, two hundred thousand naira!
2. [straight away, louder] I said Barrister Kemi Oladipo, two hundred thousand! Clap for her!
3. Chief Emeka Okonkwo is adding another fifty thousand naira.
4. Mr. Obinna Nwosu, thirty thousand naira.
5. A son of the soil, five hundred thousand naira.
6. Mama Blessing Okoro, one bag of cement.
7. Hajia Aisha Bello, quarter of a million naira.
8. Deacon Chinedu Okoro, ten thousand naira.

## Script C — learning a name (one continuous recording)

1. Chief Nwachukwu Ezenwa, one hundred thousand naira!
2. [pause ten seconds] Prof. Yemi Olatunji, forty thousand naira.
3. [pause ten seconds] Sister Chioma Nwankwo, fifteen thousand naira.
4. [pause ten seconds] Mrs. Bukola Ajayi, sixty thousand naira.
5. [pause ten seconds] Chief Nwachukwu Ezenwa is adding another fifty thousand naira.

Line 1 and line 5 must be at least forty seconds apart. Chief Nwachukwu Ezenwa
is **not** on the guest list: the runner acts as the usher and adds him as a
walk-in as soon as line 1 is flagged, while the same listening session is
still open, then records how line 5 was heard.
