For your guests

A voice agent that knows whose pledge it is.

On their own private page, a guest can simply talk. The AssemblyAI Voice Agent always says it is automated, confirms who it is speaking to before it mentions money, and uses real tools to open checkout, record a date, note a dispute or stop reminders.

  • ✓ It cannot change an amount. That tool does not exist.
  • ✓ A wrong person is refused before the pledge is discussed.
  • ✓ Every call and outcome lands in the activity log.
