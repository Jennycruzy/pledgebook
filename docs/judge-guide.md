# Trying Pledgebook

Open **https://pledgebook.54-154-121-30.sslip.io/**. Everything below takes
about five minutes. AssemblyAI processes real human speech; payments use
Paystack **test mode**, so no real money moves.

1. **Create an account.** Choose *Create an account*, enter your name, email, a
   password of at least 10 characters, and any organisation name. You are its
   owner.
2. **Start a sample event.** On *My events* choose *Practice with a sample
   event*. It has three invented guests and deletes itself after 24 hours.
   (A real event starts empty: *New event*.)
3. **Add yourself as a guest.** Open *Guests*, add your name with a phone number
   and email, and tick the follow-up consent box.
4. **Go live and listen.** Press *Go live*, open *Live event* and either press
   *Start listening* and announce a pledge — *"[your name], fifty thousand
   naira"* — or press *Process the sample recording* to run the owner's real
   70-second recording. Lines arrive as *Provisional*, then become *Confirmed*
   or *Needs checking* after the Sync recheck.
5. **Review.** Open *Needs review*. Play a clip, then assign a guest, add a
   walk-in, enter an amount or mark it *Not a pledge*. *Invite an usher* shows a
   QR code an usher scans to join on their own phone with a limited role.
6. **Follow up.** Open *Follow-up* and choose *Send pledge page* → *Copy link*.
   Open the link in a private window: this is what the guest sees.
7. **As the guest:** play the moment you pledged (shown only when the clip
   contains nobody else's name), pay part of it with the test card
   `4084 0840 8408 4081`, any future expiry, CVV `408`, and return to the page to
   see the payment counted. Or choose a date, or press *Start talking* to speak
   with the disclosed voice assistant.
8. **Settle.** Back in the staff app, open *Settlement* and *Activity*, then
   *End event*.

To run locally, see the README.
