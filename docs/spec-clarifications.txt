PLEDGEBOOK — DECISIONS AND OPEN QUESTIONS FROM HARD STOP 1
Updated 2026-09-28. The voice decision below is owner-approved; the other
items remain implementation questions until their evidence is available.

1. THE FOLLOW-UP ASSISTANT'S VOICE

Law 2 and Part 16 ban synthetic voices anywhere, while Part 7 requires an
automated speaking Voice Agent. AssemblyAI's output voices generate speech.
Both requirements cannot be implemented literally at once.

Owner decision: all donor/MC/test recordings remain real human voices;
generated speech is permitted only for the clearly disclosed automated
assistant. The browser must label the assistant as automated.

Source checked 2026-09-27:
https://www.assemblyai.com/docs/voice-agents/voice-agent-api/voices
The current voice list also does not list "ivy", which the earlier report
called documented. Do not rely on that earlier voice suggestion.

2. WHEN THE TWO PASSES DISAGREE

Law 4 says any disagreement becomes a visible flag for a human. Part 5.5
allows a clear recheck to automatically correct the pledge.

Current implementation: show both readings and the proposed correction. A
changed or disputed result stays visible; a disagreement about the person,
currency, or more than one amount clears the person match and sends the line
to an usher. Whether every clear amount correction should require an usher
before the confirmed total is a Phase 9 measurement/review question.

3. TOO MANY GUEST NAMES

The spec puts names beyond the name-list limit into the situation description.
The Sync guide advises keeping lists of names in its dedicated name field.

Proposed decision: show the exact names included and the overflow visibly;
never silently truncate or pretend excluded names were provided. Decide the
selection rule before implementing larger lists.
Source: https://www.assemblyai.com/docs/sync-stt/prompting-and-keyterms

4. WHAT HAPPENS WHEN DEMO CREDITS ARE EXHAUSTED

The spec says the sample recording still works after the daily cap is reached.
Processing that recording through the live services also consumes credits.

Proposed decision: after the cap, show a clearly labelled recorded walkthrough.
Disable new processing, including the sample-audio processing button. Never
replay stored results while presenting them as a new live run.

5. OPTIONAL HARD-SENTENCE AI ACCESS

Two documented candidate models were denied by the account. No supported
model has returned a completion. This is not proof all models are denied.

Proposed decision: keep the optional integration unavailable unless access is
verified. Ambiguous language goes to an usher; do not guess or silently use
another AI provider. Jenny does not need to choose a model.

These decisions are collected here for one review. They do not require
interrupting unrelated verification work.
